# Why V42 beats v37 (source audit)

* **Route library**: `public_v37_pkg/main.py` builds `_ROUTES={0:base}` and applies patches for route IDs 1..12 (13 tapes, each 719 actions). V42 (`incubated_candidates/v42_pkg/main.py:945`) decodes `_R108_DATA`, yielding 41 tapes: legacy IDs 0..12 plus 28 new IDs 100..128. `_R108_SHOP_ROUTES` maps 64 observed shop pairs to 27 distinct new plans. Thus V42 has materially broader production/action coverage.
* **Demand-aware switch at day 6**: v37 `_router` (around line 949) only maps shop pairs that include `YARN_STORE`; all other pairs fall back to route 0. V42 `_router` (lines 952-959) takes first two `unlocked_shops` at step >=144, and when neither is Yarn Store uses `_R108_SHOP_ROUTES.get(shops,100)` (new compatible plan). If Yarn Store is present it intentionally uses `_R110_OLD_SHOPS` (legacy mapping), preserving compatibility. At step >=648 both force terminal route 2. This is the central improvement: production route matches the observed market/shop demand rather than a fixed generic route.
* **Opening cash/input policy**: v37 patches every tape step 0 with BUY WHEAT 13 + 30 and SELL WHEAT 30. V42 uses BUY WHEAT 5 + 10 and SELL WHEAT 60 (`_R42_OPENING` at line 964). This reduces upfront spend by 28 wheat units and sells 30 extra units, providing earlier liquidity while preserving seed/input availability.
* **Wrapper stack**: v37 has no `_R85/_R86/_R88/_R95/_R97/_R124/_R127/_R128` layers. V42 adds these wrappers (lines ~2518 onward): R85 feed/fertilizer economics and route-based reserves; R86 next-day feed reachability; R88 animal-care phase/horizon accounting; R95 replenishment trimming; R97 wheat supply guard with capacity/budget checks; R124 opening atomic planting + seed budget + hire reserve; R127 last-hour plant drop and future shortage prevention; R128 next-day service worker/cargo swaps and sale-credit accounting. They are conservative overlays that modify only when projected constraints are satisfied, otherwise return parent action.
* **What is *not* the cause**: both files keep chassis settings `budget_guard=False`, `room_guard=False`, `clamp_sells=False`, `dead_stock=False`, `terminal_liquidation=False`, `front_run=False`; therefore gain is not from globally enabling risky Thomas guards. V42's gain is the route selector plus coordinated resource/accounting wrappers.
* **Measured evidence**: `incubated_candidates/v42_pkg/REPORT.md` records 20/20 wins vs v37 (+27,389.6 mean; min +9,849), 30/30 unseen holdout wins (+30,686 mean; min +18,382), and 90/90 unseen vs Flex/Thomas/Dmitrii (min +385); the cross-opponent panel adds 80 games (20 per opponent). All 200 recorded games completed 720 steps with no errors. This establishes local robustness, not a private leaderboard guarantee.
